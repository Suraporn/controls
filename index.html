Control performance monitoring system

I built a monitoring system for olefins and polyolefins plants. It benchmarks loops against minimum-variance control, and uses anomaly detection (AAKR, AANN, SPRT) to find and diagnose poorly performing loops.

Plant-wide visibility into which loops underperform, and why

Valves, analyzers & reliability

Valves · Patent

Diverter valve seal redesign

Silicone sealing rings in diverter valves kept failing, risking product contamination. Shorter maintenance intervals didn't fix it. I studied seal theory and helped design a new seal combining PTFE and silicone O-rings (a slip-plus-compression seal), then built a test unit to prove it.

A year with zero damaged seals · ~$120K saved · patented in Thailand under our team's name
Valves · Award

Gland packing life on angle control valves

Packing was replaced every six months, yet it was usually still in good condition. I developed a method to predict packing life from design factors, leakage relationships and diameter ratios, then verified it with statistics and a test rig.

Packing lasts 3× longer · runner-up, best innovative maintenance method, SCG Maintenance Club 2009
Valves · Materials

Slide gate valve guide strips

Damaged PTFE guide strips were a contamination risk to product. I led the investigation, traced it to the material's low heat-deflection temperature, and switched to PEEK.

No contamination since the change · ~$33K in maintenance saved
Valves · Materials

Butterfly valve seat upgrade

EPDM soft seats couldn't withstand the abrasive gas used to convey PTA, which created a contamination risk. I led the fix and upgraded the valves to metal seats.

No contamination since the upgrade · ~$24K in maintenance saved
Reliability · Cost

Local spare parts program

Original spare parts, such as PTFE valve seats, made instrument maintenance expensive. I compared material, thermal and mechanical properties, and had local alternatives tested at the national materials testing center.

120 parts qualified · spare-part costs down 68% · ~$110K/yr saved
